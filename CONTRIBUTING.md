# Contributing to The House Bdoyan

Thank you for helping complete the history of the Bdoyan family. You don't need to know anything about GitHub to contribute.

**Կարող եք գրել հայերեն, ռուսերեն կամ անգլերեն։ · Можно писать по-армянски, по-русски или по-английски.**
You can write in Armenian, Russian or English.

## Two ways to send information

1. **The easy way:** open the [website](https://harutbdo.github.io/house-bdoyan/), go to *Contribute*, fill in the form and send the prepared message by email, WhatsApp or Telegram.
2. **On GitHub:** [open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) and choose *Add family information* or *Correction*.

## What helps most

- **Your branch:** who your parents, grandparents and great-grandparents were, with years and villages if you know them.
- **Where your family lives today**, so the chapter on the Bdoyans today can rest on facts rather than estimates.
- **Old photographs and documents:** birth and marriage certificates, letters, passports, school records. A phone photo is fine.
- **Stories of the elders:** what they remembered about Pokr Khanchali, Abul, the old houses, the mill, who moved where and when.
- **Archive help:** visiting the National Archives in Yerevan or Tbilisi, reading old Russian or Georgian handwriting, or checking FamilySearch microfilms 1915055 and 1915180 for the baptismal name of Bdo.
- **Y-DNA:** Bdoyan men willing to take a Y-DNA test can help prove whether the Pokr Khanchali and Abul lines share one founder.

Please say **where your information comes from**: a document, a gravestone, a relative's memory (whose?), or your own memory. The book marks every fact as ● confirmed by a record, ◐ probable, or ○ family tradition, so the source matters as much as the fact.

## Living people and privacy

- Only include living people who have agreed to be named.
- Phone numbers, email addresses and home addresses are never published.
- You choose how you are credited: full name, initials, or "a member of the family".
- Anyone can ask for information about themselves to be changed or removed; open an issue or write to the compiler.

GitHub issues are public. If something is private, use the form on the website instead, which goes only to the compiler.

## Corrections

If you find a mistake in a name, date, place or translation, open a *Correction* issue and say which chapter and language it is in, what it says now, and what it should say.

## Editing the site directly

If you are comfortable with GitHub, you can also edit `index.html` and open a pull request. Each language is a separate block of the page (`data-l="hy"`, `data-l="en"`, `data-l="ru"`), so a change to the text usually needs to be made in all three.
