THE HOUSE BDOYAN

In 1829 the families of Dinarigom, a village on the high plain of Karin, packed their carts and followed the Russian army north across a new frontier. On the shore of Lake Khanchali they rebuilt their village — and one household, known as “Bdo’s people”, became the Bdoyans.

This book follows them from Bdo (Bedros), head of a great Karin household, through Bdoyents Papo and his mill, the five Bdoyan houses of the 1910s and the ethnographer Vard Bdoyan, to the 1,400 Bdoyans of today — with every fact tied to its source.
