Share What You Find

Կիսվեք ձեր գտածով

Every branch remembers a different part of the family. The book can only be completed if what each branch knows comes together in one place.

What to send

  • Your branch: the house it comes from, the oldest ancestor you know, and where its households live today (Worksheet 3).
  • Names and dates for any generation, each with its source: an elder’s memory, a certificate, a register entry with its archive reference, a gravestone.
  • Stories, memories and songs, with who told them and when.
  • Photographs and documents: a scan or a clear phone photo, with who and what is in them.
  • Corrections to anything in the book, with the reason.
  • An offer to help: visiting an archive in Yerevan or Tbilisi, reading Russian or Georgian records, or taking a DNA test.

How to send it

The online edition of The House Bdoyan has a Contribute section. Fill in the form, and the page writes your entry as a message you can send by email, WhatsApp, Viber or Telegram: {{WEBLINK}}.

How a finding becomes part of the bookEach new fact is checked against its source, given a mark (● confirmed by a record, ◐ probable, ○ family tradition), added to the family chart and the who’s who, and credited to the person who sent it in the next edition.
