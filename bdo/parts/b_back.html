Family and maps

Family tradition, as given by the Bdoyan family for this booklet: Bdo as founder, his house in Dinarigom, and the descent of all Bdoyans from him.

Map data: Natural Earth (public domain), 1:10m and 1:50m cultural and physical vectors. Historical frontier, routes and family centres drawn for this booklet; the small lakes of Javakheti are shown schematically.

A note on certaintyConfirmed by records: Pokr Khanchali’s settlement from Dinarigom in 1829–1830; Bdoyents Papo and his mill; the five Bdoyan houses of the 1910s; the Abul line from Hakob Bdoyan of Umudum; Vard Bdoyan’s life and work; the church history; the archive references. Family tradition: Bdo himself, his house in Dinarigom, and the descent of every Bdoyan, the Abul line included, from him. Estimates: Bdo’s baptismal name (probably Bedros, that is Petros), his dates, generation counts, Papo’s generation, Varvare as Vard’s mother, the likely centres on the maps. All can be tested against the records in the research handbook.
