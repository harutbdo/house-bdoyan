How to read this book

Each fact is tied to its source by a small number that leads to the Notes at the end of the book. Where it matters how sure a statement is, it carries a mark:

● confirmed by a record◐ probable, inferred from the records○ family tradition or an estimate

The founder is called Bdo (Bedros). Bdo is the name the family remembers; Bedros, the Western Armenian form of Petros, is his probable baptismal name and still has to be confirmed by a record (Chapter I).

Spelling

Armenian names are written in a simple English form based on Eastern Armenian pronunciation (Bdoyan, Khanchali, Yesoyan); Western forms such as Bedros are given where they matter. Authors keep the spelling used in their own publications (Karapetian). The names of people and places in Armenian, Georgian and Russian script are listed on the page “Names in four scripts”.
