At a glance KEY FINDINGS

The Story in Ten Facts

Հիմնական փաստերը
  1. ●The Bdoyans of Pokr Khanchali descend from families who came from Dinarigom, a village 15 km north of Erzurum (Karin), in 1829–1830.
  2. ○The family’s founder was Bdo, head of a great household in Dinarigom, born around 1740–1780.
  3. ◐“Bdo” was a pet name. His baptismal name was most likely Bedros (Petros); a register entry is still needed to prove it.
  4. ●They left with the great exodus that followed the Treaty of Adrianople of 1829, led by Archbishop Karapet Bagratuni.
  5. ●In the 1830s the Bdoyans’ patriarch in Pokr Khanchali, Bdoyents Papo, built the village’s first water-mill.
  6. ●In the 1910s the family lived wall to wall in five adjoining houses: those of Harutyun, Grigor, Anton and Davit, Mesrop and Zakar.
  7. ●The ethnographer Vard Harutyuni Bdoyan (1910–1986) was born in Pokr Khanchali; his life’s work on Javakhk was published in 2014 and names many of his relatives.
  8. ●A second line lived in Abul: Hakob Bdoyan came there from Umudum, near Dinarigom, in 1830. ○ That it shares Bdo as founder is the family’s view, still to be proven.
  9. ●About 1,440 Bdoyans live today, almost all in Georgia, Armenia and Russia (an estimate).
  10. ●The church registers of Pokr Khanchali survive in the National Archives of Armenia and on FamilySearch microfilm 1915055 and 1915180 — the next place to look for Bdo’s real name.

● confirmed by a record   ◐ probable   ○ family tradition or estimate. Sources for each fact are in the chapters and their notes.
