Reference WORDS TO KNOW

Words to Know

Բառարան
aplaապլա — “elder sister”; a respectful title for an older woman, as in Anush apla.
azgազգ — a clan or extended family line; in Javakhk, the families of one kin quarter.
Bardzr HaykԲարձր Հայք — “High Armenia”, the historic province around Karin (Erzurum).
-ents-ենց — “of the house of”; Bdoyents means “Bdo’s people”.
family lists (posemeinye spiski) — Russian household registers of 1886, naming every household head and his family.
fedayiֆիդայի — Armenian irregular fighter of the national movement, c. 1890–1915.
fond — an archive collection; references such as “fond 53, series 1, file 3848” point to one file in it.
gerdastanգերդաստան — the large patriarchal family of several generations under one roof.
gomasenyakգոմասենյակ — the raised winter room inside the cattle barn.
hampaհամփա — a haymaking team formed by kinship.
JavakhkՋավախք — the Armenian name of the Javakheti plateau, around Akhalkalaki and Ninotsminda in Georgia.
KarinԿարին — the Armenian name of Erzurum and its plain.
kin quarter — a group of joined houses where one clan lived together.
mahla, maylaմահլա — a village quarter, named after the clan’s eldest; veri mayla the upper, tsatsi mayla the lower half.
metrical book — a parish register of baptisms, marriages and burials.
nahapetնահապետ — patriarch, the head of a whole family line.
taghթաղ — the old Armenian word for a quarter, already forgotten in Karin.
tanuter, tantikinտանուտեր, տանտիկին — master and mistress of the house.
tohmտոհմ — a lineage or house descended in the male line from one ancestor.
tohmanunտոհմանուն — the name of a tohm, taken from its founder; Bdoyan began as one (Afterword).
tohmatsarտոհմածառ — family tree.
uyezd — a district of the Russian Empire, such as the Akhalkalaki uyezd.
-yan-յան — the surname ending “of”; Bdoyan, “of Bdo’s family”.
yot portյոթ պորտ — “seven navels”, the seven generations one was expected to know.
