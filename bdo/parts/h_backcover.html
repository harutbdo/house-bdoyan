FINDING THE BDOYANS

The church registers of Pokr Khanchali survive. So do the first Russian household lists, the census of 1897 and the notebooks of Vard Bdoyan. Somewhere in them is Bdo’s baptismal name, and the names of the two generations still missing from the family chart.

This handbook lists every archive, library and website that holds a piece of the Bdoyan story, explains how to ask for each record and how to read it, and gives the sheets for collecting what every branch knows.
