What this handbook is for

The House Bdoyan tells the family’s story as far as the sources reach today. This handbook is the working half: where every record is kept, how to ask for it, what to look for in it, and printable sheets for collecting what each branch knows. Every web address in it is a link.

How to use it

Start with Chapter I and follow the steps in order: the living first, then the Soviet records, then the church registers, then the first Russian lists. Chapters II and III are the full list of sources, online and on paper. Chapter IV holds the sheets: print as many as you need.

Share what you find

Send each finding, with its source, so it can go into the next edition of the book. The online edition has a Contribute section that turns your answers into a ready message: {{WEBLINK}}. Or write to {{EMAIL}}.

Privacy

Write down living people only with their agreement, and share their dates and addresses only with the family. The book names living people only where a published source already does.
